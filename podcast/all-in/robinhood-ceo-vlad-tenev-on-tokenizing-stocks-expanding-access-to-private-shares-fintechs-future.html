Questions and answers
How has Robinhood's stock performed recently, and what is the current state of its subscriber base for Robinhood Gold?
Robinhood's stock has surged 180% this year after nearly doubling in 2024, and is up over 400% in the last year. Robinhood Gold has hit a record 3.5 million subscribers.
What was the core idea Vlad Tenev pitched for Robinhood, and what was the initial business model?
The core idea was to get millennials and Gen Z to trade stocks, and the initial business model was to let them trade for free.
What was the purpose of Robinhood's "To Catch a Token" event in the south of France, and what assets did they tokenize first?
The event aimed to demonstrate what Robinhood's platform would look like if built on crypto technology, showcasing stocks on blockchains and making inaccessible assets available. They were the first to tokenize OpenAI and SpaceX.
How does Robinhood tokenize assets like private company stocks, and what is the underlying mechanism?
Robinhood tokenizes assets in a way similar to stablecoins, by keeping the underlying assets (like dollars or securities) in a reserve and then minting and burning tokens against that reserve on a one-to-one basis, allowing the tokens to trade publicly on blockchains.
What is Vlad Tenev's vision for Robinhood's future role in the financial landscape, particularly regarding wealth transfer?
Vlad envisions Robinhood as a comprehensive financial platform where customers put all their money, including direct deposits, and can bring their family members onto the platform. He believes Robinhood is well-positioned to benefit significantly from the over $130 trillion in wealth expected to transfer from older generations to younger ones.
What specific regulatory changes or support does Robinhood need from the U.S. government to advance its tokenization and broader accessibility goals?
Robinhood needs a relaxation of accreditation standards towards more self-certification, potentially through a test or a simple statement from users acknowledging risks, and consideration for extending access to individual retirement accounts.
What is "Harmonic," the company Vlad Tenev started separately from Robinhood, and what is its primary goal?
Harmonic is a company started by Vlad Tenev, completely separate from Robinhood, with the goal of building "mathematical super intelligence" that exceeds human capability, particularly for mathematical reasoning.
Why does Vlad Tenev believe broad-based ownership in AI companies is crucial, and what problem does it aim to solve?
He believes it's crucial because many AI companies are reaching massive valuations with zero retail ownership, and this technology could disrupt normal lives. Broad-based ownership would allow more people to participate in the economic boom, turning potential fear into a desire for AI's success.
How does the "Harmonic" project aim to address the issue of "hallucination" and verification in current AI models like LLMs?
Harmonic focuses on formal verification, allowing it to precisely verify if a statement is true, which provides a strong reward signal for reinforcement learning. This approach aims to solve the problem of LLMs hallucinating and the scalability challenge of human verification for LLM-generated code.
What is Robinhood's approach to consumer protection and responsibility when offering sophisticated trading options to young people?
Vlad Tenev is generally averse to controlling risk for individuals, believing that if options are available to wealthy people, they should be available to retail investors. However, he supports "reasonable things" like making it clear what one is investing in, and Robinhood has implemented educational tests before users can engage in sophisticated activities like shorting or options trading.
