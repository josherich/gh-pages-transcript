Questions and answers
According to Tulsi Gabbard, what was the fundamental purpose of the "Russiagate hoax" as revealed by the declassified documents?
The Russiagate hoax was a manipulation of intelligence designed to show something not reflective of the truth, weaponized to undermine President Trump's presidency, challenge the 2016 election outcome, and usurp the will of the people who elected him.
How did the intelligence community's assessments of Russia's intent to interfere in the 2016 U.S. election change before and after President Trump's victory?
Before the election, the intelligence community almost uniformly assessed that Putin did not have the intent or capability to hack the outcome. However, post-election, despite an initial briefing consistent with no impact, the narrative was reversed to state Russia meddled to help Trump.
What evidence of political interference was uncovered regarding the President's Daily Brief on December 8th, 2016?
A draft of the President's Daily Brief, which stated there was no interference in the outcome of the election by Russia, was pulled back and never published hours before it was to reach President Obama's desk.
What was unusual about the tasking given by the Obama administration to DNI James Clapper following the National Security Council meeting on December 9th, 2016, concerning Russia?
The tasking was not to investigate if Russia meddled in the election, but rather to "Provide me with an assessment saying how Russia meddled in the election," indicating a predetermined conclusion.
Beyond undermining Trump, what other motivations were suggested for certain Democratic leaders pushing the Russiagate narrative?
Motivations included protecting the American people from a president they did not approve of, and also to provide an external explanation for Hillary Clinton's loss rather than blaming the Democratic Party's choice of nominee.
How does Tulsi Gabbard reconcile the idea of "Russiagate being a hoax" with the acknowledged fact that Russia did attempt to interfere in U.S. elections?
Gabbard clarifies that while the claim of Trump being a Russian asset or colluding with Putin was a manufactured hoax, she does not dispute that Russia's intent was to "sow chaos" in the election. She acknowledges Russian interference occurred, but distinguishes it from direct collusion to elect Trump.
What core values did Tulsi Gabbard believe the Democratic Party had abandoned, leading to her departure and view of the party as "unrecognizable"?
She felt the Democratic Party had moved away from its foundational values of fighting for the "little guy" and working people, protecting the environment, and upholding free speech, even for unpopular views.
Why did the Trump administration designate certain cartels as foreign terrorist organizations, and what capabilities do these cartels possess that justify this designation?
The designation was driven by the devastating effect cartels like MS-13 and Tren de Aragua were having on U.S. communities. These cartels exhibit sophisticated capabilities, including intelligence and counterintelligence collection, and the use of armed drones for surveillance and targeting.
