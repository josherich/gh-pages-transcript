We’ve made superhuman poker AIs for No Limit Texas Hold’em.   • 5 x 1,326 = 6,630 states

What is the key difference between games like poker where Game Theory Optimal (GTO) works well and games like Diplomacy where it doesn't?
GTO works well in two-player zero-sum games like poker because it converges to a minimax equilibrium. In games like Diplomacy with collaboration and competition, a defensive GTO policy isn't useful, and you need to adapt to other players.
Why was Noam skeptical that pre-training scale alone would lead to superintelligence within economically feasible limits?
He believed a general reasoning paradigm was needed, as current LLMs lacked this despite being general. He thought you would hit economic limits before reaching superintelligence without it.
How does the "thinking fast and slow" or System One/System Two analogy apply to AI models and their scaling?
Models need a certain baseline capability (System One) from pre-training to effectively benefit from the extra "thinking" or reasoning (System Two). System Two gives a lift only if System One is sufficiently capable.
Noam mentions that researchers built complex agentic systems before reasoning models emerged. Why did reasoning models make these systems "worse"?
With reasoning models, you can often give the same question without scaffolding, and the model just does it directly. The complex scaffolding built for non-reasoning models became unnecessary and sometimes detrimental.
Despite leading in pre-training scale, why did OpenAI leadership invest research effort into RL and reasoning, according to Noam?
OpenAI leadership recognized that another paradigm beyond just pre-training was needed to reach AGI/superintelligence, and it was worth researching other directions, like RL, despite the main focus on scaling pre-training.
Why is sample efficiency a significant challenge for AI models compared to humans, particularly in games like poker?
Humans can learn player weaknesses in a dozen hands, while AIs historically needed thousands of hands (e.g., 10,000) to get a good profile, making them much less data/sample efficient.
