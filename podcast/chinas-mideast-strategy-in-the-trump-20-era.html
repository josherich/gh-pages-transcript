What does Grant Rumley describe as the potential ideal vision for the Middle East by the end of a Trump administration?
An Iran with its nuclear program boxed, free flow of commerce in the Gulf, expanded Abraham Accords including Saudi Arabia, a Trump Tower in Damascus, Syria geopolitically aligned with the West, and progress on the Palestinian front.
What are some key takeaways for China observing Trump 2.0 in the Middle East, according to Zhang Chuchu's closing remarks?
China knows Trump is unpredictable and needs its own clear strategy, it doesn't want competition but may be forced into it, the agency of regional powers is important, and the Middle East is of higher importance for the US than China.
