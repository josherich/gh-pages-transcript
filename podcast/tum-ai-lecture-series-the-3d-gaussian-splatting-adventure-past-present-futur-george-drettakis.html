Bernard introduced efficient sorting of points, which was a critical component for achieving fast rendering on the GPU.

Gaussian Splatting allows for a completely interpretable 3D representation, unlike methods heavily reliant on complex neural networks.
What is a major goal for the speaker's new ERC grant regarding 3D representation?
The goal is to develop a new 3D representation and companion rendering algorithms compatible with physically based rendering while retaining the power of neural rendering.
According to the speaker, where do traditional mesh representations fit into the future of graphics alongside new methods like Gaussian Splatting?
Meshes still have a bright future due to existing pipelines, workflows, and their compatibility with physically-based rendering features that are currently difficult with splats.
